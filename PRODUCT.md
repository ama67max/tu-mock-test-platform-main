# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

TU entrance aspirants preparing to crack Tribhuvan University entrance exams.

## Product Purpose

TU Mock Test helps students prepare under realistic exam conditions through timed mock exams, score breakdowns, and subject-level practice. Success means giving students a dependable way to build speed, accuracy, and confidence before the real entrance exam.

## Positioning

The platform combines realistic exam simulation with offline continuity, so practice can continue when a student's connection cannot.

## Operating Context

Students use the platform for repeated practice across TU entrance tracks, often on personal laptops or mobile devices and sometimes with unreliable connectivity. They move between exam attempts, review, dashboards, leaderboards, and saved offline work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the existing routes, application behavior, exam workflows, offline/PWA support, and existing product copy unless a change is necessary for clarity or accessibility.

## Brand Commitments

The existing monochrome visual direction is a binding product identity. The interface should feel focused, credible, and quietly confident, with room for distinctive frontend craft that improves conversion without becoming distracting.

## Evidence on Hand

The working React/Vite application, existing page implementations under `frontend/src/pages`, shared styles under `frontend/src/styles`, and PWA/offline features are the source of truth. Do not fabricate customer, performance, or outcome claims.

## Product Principles

- Make realistic practice feel immediately understandable.
- Turn every attempt into a useful next step.
- Keep study momentum resilient to connectivity.
- Favor clear, accessible interfaces over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Improve keyboard accessibility, focus visibility, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion support while preserving the existing workflows.
