Describe the process of oxidative phosphorylation in the inner mitochondrial membrane, including the role of the proton gradient.

Correct Answer (Summary)

Electrons from NADH and FADH2 are transferred through the ETC, pumping protons to create a gradient. Proton flow back through ATP synthase drives ATP synthesis.
